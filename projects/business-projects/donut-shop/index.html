Our little corner of the neighborhood

A good day starts here.

Daybreak began with a simple idea: make the kind of donut worth getting out of bed for. We mix and shape each batch by hand, let the dough rise slowly overnight, and fry everything fresh before the doors open. Good ingredients, a little patience, and a warm welcome are our recipe.
